Privacy Policy

ProjectSync is intended for personal desktop use. It uses rclone and Google OAuth to access Google Drive files authorized by the user for listing, comparison and requested transfers.

Project files, metadata and authentication credentials are processed locally and by Google Drive as needed for these operations. The informational website does not request Google credentials or project files. No separate ProjectSync data collection server is intended.

Users can revoke access in their Google Account security settings and remove local rclone credentials. Google and GitHub may process data under their own privacy policies.
